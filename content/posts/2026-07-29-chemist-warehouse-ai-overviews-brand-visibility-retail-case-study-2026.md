Chemist Warehouse grew its appearances in Google AI Overviews by 220% in a single quarter, alongside a 50% lift in organic page views. The figures, disclosed at an industry panel in late July 2026, are among the first hard numbers brands have put against AI-visibility work. They also come with an admission worth more than the win: no one on the panel could tie that visibility to a sale.

## By the numbers

- **220%** quarterly increase in Chemist Warehouse or its products surfaced in Google AI Overviews ([PPC Land](https://ppc.land/chemist-warehouse-gains-220-more-ai-overview-appearances-in-a-quarter/)).
- **50%** increase in average organic page views over the same quarter.
- **46%** increase in brand mentions across ChatGPT and Google AI Mode over five months, for a separate unnamed Australian retailer.
- Mention-to-cited-source overlap falls as low as **30%** on Gemini across 1,200+ brands ([Semrush AI Visibility Index](https://ai-visibility-index.semrush.com/)).
- **39.8%** drop in outbound organic clicks when a Google AI Overview appears ([randomized field experiment, Agarwal & Sen](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/google-ai-overviews-study-finds-lost-clicks-werent-lower-quality/581298/)).

## What Chemist Warehouse actually did

The disclosure came from a panel at IAB Australia's Commerce and Retail Media Summit, published as a recording on 28 July 2026. Jennifer Stokes, Head of Retail Media at Suddenly — the content agency inside News Corp — walked through the work behind the number. It was not a technical SEO sprint. It was a content property.

Chemist Warehouse runs a multi-channel brand called the House of Wellness: a quarterly magazine, a monthly newspaper-inserted magazine distributing 5.3 million copies a month, a website vertical produced with Body and Soul, a television programme, and a radio station. Every digital asset is optimised for discovery at the point of creation. The result Stokes described was not just traffic — it was repositioning. The work moved Chemist Warehouse, in her framing, "from being understood as a pharmacy to being part of a health and wellness journey."

That distinction matters. The brand did not chase AI Overview placement directly. It built genuinely useful, wide-distribution content across owned and publisher channels, and machine visibility followed. As Stokes put it, the content "earns attention because it is genuine, authentic, valuable content to the customers, but we've made it discoverable at the same time."

Sophie Miura, General Manager eCommerce at News Australia, connected the two directly: message consistency across owned assets and publisher content is what determines whether a brand surfaces coherently across models. Inconsistent signals across touchpoints weaken the base of evidence a model draws on. Both teams, in other words, won the same way — by saturating the sources AI engines read, not by gaming a single surface.

## The visibility number is real. The revenue number is missing.

Here is the part most brand write-ups will skip. Miura was blunt about the gap between mentions and money: "There is no clear framework at this stage for tying that piece together. Like I'll be completely transparent." A 220% lift in AI Overview appearances and a 46% lift in brand mentions describe presence in an answer — not a transaction.

The wider evidence base agrees. The channel is unmistakably real and growing — [Adobe data](https://www.semrush.com/news/463141-semrush-releases-expanded-2026-ai-visibility-index-analyzing-126-million-ai-search-prompts/) shows AI-referred traffic to US retail sites up 1,324% between October 2024 and May 2026 — yet 45% of marketing leaders in the same research say they cannot accurately measure their brand's visibility inside AI answers. And the click economics underneath are deteriorating: a pre-registered randomized field experiment by Saharsh Agarwal (Indian School of Business) and Ananya Sen (Carnegie Mellon) found that when an AI Overview appears, outbound organic clicks fall 39.8% and zero-click searches rise 34.5% — with no measurable drop in click quality. Winning the answer and losing the click are now the same event.

## Why the chain breaks at the first link

The mention-to-conversion chain has a structural weakness that predates any single brand's measurement problem: an AI engine will frequently name a brand while citing entirely different domains as its evidence. Semrush's analysis of 126 million US AI search prompts across more than 1,200 brands found the overlap between the brands an engine mentions and the sources it actually cites ranges from 64% on Google AI Overviews down to roughly 30% on Gemini.

That gap is the whole game. A brand can appear in the answer while third-party sources supply the proof the model trusts — which means the durable asset is not the mention, it is the [earned authority](https://machinerelations.ai/glossary/earned-authority) sitting behind it. This is the discipline [Jaxon Parrott named Machine Relations](https://jaxonparrott.com/blog/when-ai-stops-being-theoretical): making a brand legible and credible to the machines that now mediate discovery. The House of Wellness result is a textbook case of it — broad, corroborated, third-party-carried presence rather than a single optimised page.

## What actually drove the gain

Strip the retail-media vocabulary away and the Chemist Warehouse story is a corroboration story. The brand fed consistent, high-quality signals into many independent surfaces — its own magazines, a publisher partnership, television, radio — and the engines rewarded the density. Research on [earned versus owned citation rates](https://machinerelations.ai/research/earned-vs-owned-ai-citation-rates-2026) shows AI engines cite independent sources at several times the rate of a company's own pages, which is exactly why a multi-channel content property outperforms a homepage rewrite. The lesson is not "make content discoverable." It is that [AI visibility](https://machinerelations.ai/glossary/ai-visibility) is won at the source layer, across independent publishers, not at the owned-page layer. Chemist Warehouse had a distribution advantage most brands lack — 5.3 million monthly copies and a publisher partner — and it converted that reach into machine legibility. Brands without that footprint have to earn the same corroboration deliberately.

## What CMOs should take from this

Three moves separate the durable read of this case from the celebratory one. First, treat visibility figures as leading indicators, not outcomes — a 220% lift in appearances is a real signal, but book it as reach, not revenue, until the conversion framework exists. Second, invest where the engines actually draw evidence: independent, trusted publications, not another owned blog post, because the mention you want is carried by the sources you don't own. Third, measure the source layer, not the screenshot — track which outlets cite your brand and how consistently the major engines surface it across real buyer questions.

## FAQ

### How did Chemist Warehouse grow its AI Overview appearances 220%?
Through a multi-channel content property, the House of Wellness, spanning magazines, a publisher-partnered website vertical, television, and radio — all optimised for discovery. According to the panel that disclosed it, the gain came from broad, consistent, high-quality content across owned and publisher channels, not from optimising a single page.

### Does a rise in AI Overview appearances mean more sales?
Not necessarily. The executives who disclosed the 220% figure said openly there is no established framework tying AI visibility to conversion, and Adobe research found 45% of brands still cannot fully measure AI visibility. A randomized field experiment also found AI Overviews cut outbound organic clicks 39.8% when shown, so appearing in an answer can coincide with losing the click.

### Why does an AI engine mention a brand but cite different sources?
Because mention and citation are separate mechanics. Semrush's analysis of 126 million prompts found mention-to-cited-source overlap as low as 30% on Gemini. An engine can name a brand while drawing its evidence from independent third-party domains — which is why earned coverage in trusted publications is the more durable asset.

### What should a brand measure instead of AI Overview appearances alone?
The source layer. Track which independent publications AI engines cite for your category, and measure how consistently the major engines surface your brand across many realistic buyer prompts. Presence in one answer is a sample of one; persistence across sources and prompts is the asset.
